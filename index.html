Living Traditions

Explore Odisha's Culture & Heritage

An unbroken tapestry of classical dance, mythological scroll art, grand chariot festivals, and world-renowned temple cuisine.

Odissi Dance

One of India's classical dance forms with roots in temple traditions dating back 2,000 years. Renowned for sculptural tribhangi poses, lyrical grace, and devotional storytelling.

Learn More →

Pattachitra Art

Intricate cloth-based scroll paintings prepared with 100% natural organic pigments, illustrating mythological epics, flora, and fauna in the heritage village of Raghurajpur.

Learn More →

Rath Yatra of Puri

The grand chariot festival of Lord Jagannath, Balabhadra, and Subhadra — the world's most massive annual chariot procession drawing millions of pilgrims to the Grand Road.

Learn More →

Odia Temple Cuisine

Savour sacred Puri Mahaprasad cooked in earthen pots atop wood fires, aromatic Dalma lentils, Pakhala fermented rice, and the iconic caramelised cottage cheese dessert Chhena Poda.

Learn More →
