Heritage & Traditions

Explore Odisha's Culture

A tapestry of art, dance, festivals and culinary traditions spanning millennia

Odissi Dance

One of India's classical dance forms with roots in temple traditions dating back 2,000 years. Known for sculpturesque poses and graceful movements.

Learn More →

Pattachitra Art

Intricate cloth-based scroll paintings depicting mythological stories, renowned for rich colors, creative motifs, and fine brushwork.

Learn More →

Rath Yatra

The grand chariot festival of Lord Jagannath in Puri — a world-famous annual pilgrimage drawing millions of devotees every year.

Learn More →

Odia Cuisine

Savour the divine Mahaprasad of Puri, traditional Dalma, Chhena Poda and aromatic Pakhala — a culinary heritage unique to Odisha.

Why Odisha?

The Soul of Incredible India

Odisha offers an unmatched blend of ancient civilisation, natural wonder and living culture

01

1,500+ Temples

Bhubaneswar, the Temple City of India, has an unbroken lineage of Kalinga architecture spanning over 1,400 years.

02

450 km Coastline

From Olive Ridley turtle nesting sites at Gahirmatha to the serene beaches of Puri and Gopalpur.

03

Rich Biodiversity

Home to Royal Bengal Tigers, Irrawaddy Dolphins, Saltwater Crocodiles and over 500 species of birds.

04

Living Traditions

Pattachitra, Tribal art, Sambalpuri weaving, Dhokra crafts — living traditions passed down through generations.
